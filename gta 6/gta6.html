DATA INSIGHTS & FAQ

What is the fastest vehicle in the Leonida Registry?

Currently, the Pegassi Zentorno leads the registry with a verified top speed of 213 MPH. Performance metrics are updated weekly based on telemetry data from the Leonida sector.

How are weapon damage ratings calculated?

Damage (DMG) ratings are normalized on a 10-point scale based on ballistic impact tests against standard armor plating. The Heavy Rifle currently holds a 7.2/10 rating.

Are these assets available for procurement?

Procurement costs (¢) are estimated market values. Restricted items, marked with the red 'RESTRICTED' tag, require specific clearance levels for legal acquisition.

How often is the database indexed?

The GTA STACK database performs a full sector sweep every 24 hours. New additions are highlighted in the 'Recent Additions' ledger at the bottom of the registry.
